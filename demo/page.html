llm-url-scan: live local demo

Ten made-up pages served inside a local test network. Press Scan and the real service captures the page in its isolated browser and asks the real model whether it looks like phishing. The answer is advisory only.

Every scan calls the real Anthropic API and costs about USD 0.01 (a little more when the second model also runs). Pages that cannot be read cost nothing.

The expected outcome on each card is a design, not a guarantee. Ten made-up pages show nothing about accuracy: a match does not prove the service is good, and a miss does not prove it is bad.

Ready. One scan runs at a time.
